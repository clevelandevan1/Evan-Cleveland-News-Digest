Nvidia in talks for up to $250B backstop on OpenAI's $500B Ohio data center

↪ Continues Tuesday's hyperscaler capex thread — what was a 'possible' Nvidia guarantee is now reported with specific dollar figures and drawing its first public financing-structure criticism.

Nvidia is reportedly discussing a roughly $250 billion financial guarantee to support OpenAI's lease of a 10-gigawatt, $500 billion AI data center campus in Ohio being built by SoftBank's SB Energy. Because OpenAI lacks an investment-grade credit rating, Nvidia's backing would help SB Energy raise debt on better terms; Nvidia is separately weighing financing OpenAI's chip purchases worth up to $350 billion more. Analysts have flagged the arrangement as 'circular financing' that blurs the line between genuine demand and vendor-funded demand.

What’s new Confirms and sizes the financing floated in earlier reports as roughly $250B for the Ohio lease, plus a separate, larger $350B facility for chip purchases — and surfaces the first wave of 'circular financing' criticism from analysts.
How outlets frame it CNBC and Taipei Times frame this as a financing-mechanics story (credit ratings, debt terms); Al Jazeera leans more on the scale and ambition of OpenAI's infrastructure buildout. None of the three take a position on whether the arrangement is sound.
Context

SB Energy is SoftBank's energy-infrastructure arm building the Ohio data center campus. OpenAI, as a private company without its own investment-grade rating, needs a well-rated backer like Nvidia to help the project's debt get favorable terms — the same basic mechanism used across the AI buildout, where chipmakers increasingly help finance the very customers buying their chips.
