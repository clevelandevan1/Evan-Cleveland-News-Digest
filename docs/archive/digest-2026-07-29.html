New round of networking and OS vulnerabilities to patch

Cisco Talos says a state-aligned actor (UAT-8616) has exploited seven Cisco SD-WAN zero-days in 2026, chaining an authentication-bypass flaw with a privilege-escalation bug to gain root access on government and critical-infrastructure devices. Separately, Palo Alto Networks patched 13 PAN-OS vulnerabilities, the most severe a high-severity buffer overflow in its User-ID Terminal Server Agent, and Apple shipped fixes for 187 vulnerabilities across iOS 26.6, iPadOS 26.6 and macOS Tahoe 26.6. None of the Palo Alto or Apple flaws are reported as actively exploited; the Cisco campaign is.

What’s new Clusters three separate but concurrent patch/exploitation stories into one action item: an actively-exploited Cisco campaign versus routine (so far unexploited) Palo Alto and Apple patches.
How outlets frame it Cybersecurity Dive centers the story on threat-actor attribution and the ongoing exploitation risk. SecurityWeek's coverage of Palo Alto and Apple is more routine vulnerability-disclosure reporting, without alarm framing since neither is known to be exploited.
